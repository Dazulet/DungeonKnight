Audio

All sound effects (Sfx) and both music loops (Music) are original files made by
procedural synthesis with Tools/generate_audio.py. See README.md, "Audio files and
licences", for the list of files. GameMixer.mixer is the AudioMixer (Master, Music,
Music Pause, SFX; snapshots Normal and Paused). It is created by the menu item
Dungeon Knight > Build Sound Pass if it is missing.
